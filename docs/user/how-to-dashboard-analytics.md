# How to read your dashboard

> **For developers:** [Dashboard Analytics implementation](../dev/dashboard-analytics-implementation.md) · **All guides:** [Documentation home](../README.md)

The Dashboard is your spending at a glance: totals, trends, where your money goes, and what you spent most recently.

---

## Open the Dashboard

Click **Dashboard** in the navigation, or the Spendwise logo.

![The full dashboard](../images/dashboard-analytics/dashboard.png)

## The four summary cards

| Card | What it tells you |
|---|---|
| **Total spending** | Everything you've ever recorded, and how many expenses that is |
| **This month** | What you've spent since the 1st. The colored badge compares it with all of last month: **red ▲** means you spent more, **green ▼** means less. |
| **Daily average** | This month's spending divided by the number of days so far |
| **Top category** | Where most of this month's money went |

![Summary cards with the month-over-month badge](../images/dashboard-analytics/summary-cards.png)

> The comparison badge appears only once you have expenses from last month.

## Monthly spending chart

Shows your total for each of the last six months; the current month's label is in bold.

- **Hover** over a month (or press **Tab** to move between them) to see the exact amount.

![Monthly chart with a tooltip](../images/dashboard-analytics/monthly-chart.png)

## By category

Shows how your spending splits across categories, largest first, with each one's amount and percentage.

- Use the **Month / All time** switch in the corner to change the period.

![Category breakdown](../images/dashboard-analytics/by-category.png)

## Recent expenses

Your six latest expenses. Use the pencil and trash icons to edit or delete one, or click **View all** to open the full list.

## FAQ

**The dashboard is empty.**
You haven't added any expenses yet. Click **Add your first expense**, or **Load sample data** to explore with realistic examples.

**Why is "This month" lower than I expect?**
It only counts expenses dated this calendar month. Check the dates on recent entries.

**Can I see more than six months?**
Not on the chart yet. Use [search and filtering](how-to-expense-search-filtering.md) with a custom date range to see totals for any period.
